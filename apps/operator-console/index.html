ADMINISTRATOR GUIDANCE

Feature readiness & risk centre

Read-only guidance · no enable switches

Review what each capability does, its current implementation state and the approvals required. This preview does not grant permissions or change tenant/device policy.

Collective Defence — high privacy impact · default off

Correlates privacy-minimised security signals across explicitly enrolled devices. Requires independent tenant administrator opt-in and individual device-owner opt-in. Revocation must stop future sharing. It does not grant remote control or execution.

Security Centre orchestration — high impact · read-only foundations

Coordinates status from customer-selected antivirus, firewall and network-security tools. Current integrations are limited; no firewall rules, files, processes or endpoint settings are changed by this preview.

Model distribution — supply-chain and resource risk · not enabled

Future administrator allowlists must pin the exact artifact digest, publisher, licence and size, then check each device's fresh RAM/storage/runtime fit and owner consent. This catalogue does not download or distribute models.

Remote Worker / Partner execution — critical risk · blocked

Would delegate approved workloads to enrolled devices. It remains disabled until production identity, authenticated transport, receiver-side authorization, OS-enforced isolation, resource limits, cancellation, revocation and independent security review are complete. No administrator override is available.

Trusted-device enrollment and approvalContract / prototype

Device identity and signed approval primitives exist, but production identity hosting and key custody are not complete.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Authenticated OIDC and HTTPS service.
  • Protected KMS/HSM or equivalent key custody and authoritative revocation.
  • Tenant-isolation, key-rotation and customer deployment validation.
Authenticated leases and reservationsLocal / durable foundations

Lease and reservation policy primitives do not constitute a distributed production scheduler or authorize dispatch.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Production service-side and receiver-side authorization.
  • Replay protection, atomic reservation, expiry and cancellation.
  • Multi-device concurrency, opt-out race and recovery tests.
Workload admission and resource utilizationPolicy contracts only

Resource policies are advisory until every worker rechecks fresh device evidence and consent at admission time.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Fresh RAM, storage, power, thermal and interactive-use evidence.
  • Separate device-owner opt-in, administrator schedule and resource quotas.
  • OS-enforced isolation, cancellation and audit before any dispatch.
Enterprise Intelligence plannerDesign only

No production provider connectors or account integrations are established by this operator preview.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Tenant policy plus per-user delegated OAuth consent.
  • Read-only source access, least-privilege scopes and revocation.
  • No message sending, calendar mutation or autonomous scheduling in the MVP.
Tenant-isolated cacheContract only

Cache isolation and authorization requirements are documented; a production tenant-scoped cache service is not established by this preview.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Partition by tenant, site, device, authenticated principal, scope and policy version.
  • Re-authorize every lookup against authoritative service state.
  • Prove revocation, role-change and tenant-switch invalidation with cross-tenant tests.
Infrastructure Shield profilesDesign only

Customer-selected firewall, monitoring and legacy-network profiles are deployment designs, not an installed or reconfigured service.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Customer-selected tools and explicit scope; no silent installation.
  • Least-privilege deployment, signed configuration, rollback and operator handover.
  • Controlled customer-lab validation before any production network changes.
Self-Evolution EnginePolicy foundation · not production-wired

The policy requires administrator permission and activation, approved models and scopes, the dual-approval setting, and a trusted build environment. The generation gate does not yet verify two distinct approvals. The signed-approval verifier and action-level role/owner-opt-in gate are code foundations only; this read-only console has no activation, staging or apply workflow.

Prerequisites and evidence
  • Authenticated operator sessions and durable device-owner opt-in, with revocation checked at use.
  • Atomic durable single-use approval-nonce consumption before any follow-on change workflow; signature verification alone is not replay protection.
  • Tenant-scoped trusted source checkout and isolated build workspace.
  • Protected control-plane policy, independent review and administrator approval.
  • Organization-controlled KMS/HSM signing, staged rollout and rollback.
  • Fleet devices receive only verified signed artifacts; generated code is never executed on worker devices.

Unknown, stale or missing policy/consent must fail closed. A tenant administrator cannot override an individual device owner's consent or a missing security gate.
